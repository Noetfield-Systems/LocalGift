Terms & founding status

Current service

LocalGift is a founding-stage discovery and merchant application website. The gift-card studio is a preview only. It does not generate redeemable codes, accept consumer gift-card payments, guarantee merchant discounts or confirm that any business participates.

Merchant-issued cards

When actual listings become available, card checkout and issuance will be operated by the identified merchant or authorized card provider. That issuer determines eligible locations, redemption procedures and terms, consistent with applicable consumer law. LocalGift will not imply cards can be redeemed at unrelated stores.

Merchant submissions

Submitting an application does not create a partnership or authorize LocalGift to use your brand or issue gift cards. We may contact you about onboarding and request evidence that you control the business and official checkout.

Prices and availability

There is currently no active consumer card catalog. Any future merchant fees, commissions, gift-card amounts, promotional terms or launch offers must be agreed and disclosed before purchase or activation. No unsupported monetary or investment return is promised.
