Privacy notice

LocalGift collects information you voluntarily submit. For consumer launch notifications: your email, preferred gift category and preview amount. For merchant applications: business name, contact, email, website, city and the gift-card platform you use. We record consent and submission time.

Why we use it

To contact you about LocalGift launch information or to respond to your merchant application, validate merchant eligibility, and operate our partner directory. We do not sell your contact details or use submissions to issue stored-value cards.

Storage and controls

Applications are stored in a restricted PostgreSQL database. We use service providers for website hosting and storage. Data remains until it is no longer needed for the stated purpose, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. Please do not submit payment card details or confidential merchant credentials.

Your choices

You can request access, correction, withdrawal of marketing consent or deletion of your submitted personal information. Contact the operator through its public business website at noetfield.com. Withdrawal does not affect past lawful processing.

No consumer accounts, paid gift card purchases or stored-value balances are currently created by this website.
