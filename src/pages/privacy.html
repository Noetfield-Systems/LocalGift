LOCALGIFT / NOETFIELD SYSTEMS INC.

Privacy notice.

LocalGift is operated by Noetfield Systems Inc. This notice describes information we handle when you join the club, share a greeting, browse issuer-linked gifts, submit a merchant offer, contact a resale seller or inquire about corporate gifting.

What we collect

  • Visitor and member activity: a randomly generated device-bound member ID and referral code, an HttpOnly session cookie, associated referral attribution, noncash quest completions, issuer checkout link clicks, and activity timestamps. The session token is stored as a hash in our database and the cookie normally expires after 60 days.
  • Voluntary buyer and merchant information: names, business names, work email addresses, city, preferred category, issuer website, merchant platform, consent status, service order and gift design fields that you provide.
  • Resale: seller-provided item description, condition, asking price, city and private seller contact email; buyer inquiry message and private buyer email. These emails do not appear in the public listing API. The operator can access them to coordinate introductions.
  • Commercial verification: issuer agreements, official checkout URLs, merchant offer rates, external order references, evidence references, settlement records and internal reward accounting. These records do not by themselves establish completed payments or withdrawable cash.
  • Technical data: standard hosting/security logs and limited request-rate data used to protect the service.

How we use it

We use this information to operate the shopping directory and club, process valid applications, moderate resale listings, coordinate requested buyer/seller introductions, prevent abuse, maintain records, evaluate merchant eligibility, and account for operator-reported commercial events. We do not sell your submitted personal contact details to advertisers.

Device-bound sessions and third parties

Club access currently uses a device-bound Secure, HttpOnly session cookie without email identity verification. Clearing cookies or changing devices can make your guest account inaccessible. Public merchant checkout links open the merchant's or authorized provider's website, governed by that issuer's own terms and privacy practices. If enabled, LocalGift merchant-service payments are handled through Stripe-hosted Checkout; do not enter payment credentials into our application forms.

Sharing, retention, and your choices

We use hosting and database service providers, including Railway/PostgreSQL, to operate LocalGift. Private resale inquiry details may be shared with the buyer and seller involved if an introduction is requested. We retain records as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, security, and applicable recordkeeping duties. You can request access, correction, deletion where permitted, or withdrawal of marketing consent through Noetfield Systems Inc. We may require appropriate verification before disclosing or deleting information.

Club XP is not money. LocalGift does not currently provide a withdrawable rewards wallet, banking account, or platform escrow service.
