QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

No fine-print surprises.

Can I buy a LocalGift card today?

Not yet. This is the founding launch. The card studio is a design preview, and no third-party brand cards are listed until authorized issuers are connected. You can join the launch list or apply as a merchant now.

Who actually issues the gift card?

The participating business does, through its established Shopify or Square gift-card program or another contracted issuer. The issuing business remains responsible for the card's value, redemption and applicable terms.

Can a single card work at several businesses?

Not by default. Shopify gift cards normally work only at the issuing store, and Square cards follow the issuer's program. Multi-merchant gifting would require explicit partner and payment arrangements and will not be advertised until available.

Can a gift card expire in British Columbia?

Most gift cards with a specified dollar value sold in BC cannot expire and generally cannot carry inactivity fees. Certain types and exceptions apply. We will show each issuer's actual terms before purchase.

Can merchants issue their own branded cards?

Yes. Shopify and Square already support merchant-branded eGift cards. We help with the gift storefront, launch presentation and discovery; the merchant's official checkout and issuer system handle monetary card issuance.

Will I need an app?

No. Buyers can browse the web page; each active participating merchant provides a supported delivery and redemption method such as email and its own payment checkout.
